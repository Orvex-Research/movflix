Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Movflix ("we", "our", or "us"). We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you visit or interact with our website at https://idms.eu.cc/.

2. Information We Collect

Movflix is committed to minimal data collection. We do not require account registration or store sensitive personal information:

  • Local Storage: Your watchlist ("My List") and viewing history are stored entirely locally inside your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Cookies & Security Tokens: We issue temporary session tokens (e.g. mfx_gate) solely to secure API endpoints against unauthorized scraping and DDoS attacks.
  • Technical Logs: Standard web server logs may capture non-identifying request data (such as IP address, user-agent, request timestamp) for security and operational monitoring.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

Our service uses third-party APIs to provide streaming media discovery and metadata:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): We fetch movie titles, posters, descriptions, and ratings using the TMDB API. TMDB does not receive your personal information from our servers.
  • Media Players & CDNs: Embedded media players stream content from external hosting providers. When video streams are accessed, standard HTTP requests are handled by those external providers according to their respective policies.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption, strict Content Security Policies (CSP), and automated rate limiting to protect user sessions and server integrity.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website at https://idms.eu.cc/.
